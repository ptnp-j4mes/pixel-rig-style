# Asset attributions

## Village Pack tree

`Tree_VillageBroadleaf` is adapted from [Tree with leaves #1](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tree-with-leaves-1-d4a1988bfdb143a298f2333d4ecd6996) by [Helindu](https://sketchfab.com/Helindu), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: scaled for the village scene, bark recolored, merged into one mesh, and simplified for browser rendering while retaining the source leaf geometry and texture.

## EZ-Tree broadleaf

`Tree_EZ_OakMedium` was generated from the Oak Medium preset with [EZ-Tree](https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree) by Daniel Greenheck, using package version 1.1.0. The package is distributed with an MIT license. The exported branch and leaf meshes were combined into one static mesh and scaled to 5.5 scene units; the model retains the bundled bark and leaf materials.

## User-supplied bush variants

`Tree_Bush_01` through `Tree_Bush_07` are adapted from `tree-model/bush/tree.glb` and `tree-model/bush/tree (1).glb` through `tree-model/bush/tree (6).glb`. Their branch and leaf primitives were kept together under named tree nodes and uniformly scaled so each model's longest dimension is 5.5 scene units. The source GLBs do not contain creator or license metadata.

## Village Pack granite mountains

`Mountain_StoneRidge` and `Mountain_GraniteMassif` are original procedural models guided by the layered rock forms and color palette of [Stone Rock(part11)](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/stone-rockpart11-a2acbcb6b767467483459be6ccaa4534) by [YN14R](https://sketchfab.com/YN14R), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The source model geometry and its trees are not included. The mountain geometry and materials were created for this project.
